Waseem Shokat
PhD applicant in applied mathematics (MS Mathematics, COMSATS University Islamabad).
I work on fractional-order epidemic models and physics-informed machine learning:
building models with memory, analysing them (existence, stability, reproduction numbers),
and learning their unknown parameters from real data – with honest checks of what the data
can and cannot identify.
Research interests
Fractional differential equations (Caputo models, L1 and predictor–corrector schemes)
Physics-informed neural networks for inverse problems and parameter identification
Mathematical epidemiology: COVID-19, tuberculosis, dengue
Numerical methods for reaction–diffusion models (finite elements, meshless RBF-FD)
